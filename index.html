Who we are

Grounded in Uganda,
built for the region

Minaki Holdings is a Kampala-based holding company operating across agriculture, transportation, real estate, and construction materials. Our team works to build resilient supply chains, sustainable land investments, and dependable infrastructure solutions.

From our headquarters in Kamwokya, we serve business partners across Uganda and the East African region with quality and integrity. Every decision we make is rooted in long-term thinking and local knowledge.
